The spellbook

Harry Potter spells
and meanings

Spells work through knowledge, intent, and control. This archive groups memorable incantations by what they do, with a concise explanation of each effect.

Everyday and utility magic

Summoning charm

Accio

Summons an object to the caster.

Light charm

Lumos

Creates a light at the tip of a wand.

Counter-charm

Nox

Extinguishes the light made by Lumos.

Unlocking charm

Alohomora

Opens locked doors and similar simple locks.

Repairing charm

Reparo

Repairs a damaged object.

Levitation charm

Wingardium Leviosa

Makes an object float and move under control.

Defence and duelling

Disarming charm

Expelliarmus

Forces an opponent to release what they are holding, often a wand.

Shield charm

Protego

Creates a magical shield against many attacks.

Stunning spell

Stupefy

Stuns a target and can leave them unconscious.

Full body-bind

Petrificus Totalus

Temporarily immobilises the target’s body.

Severing charm

Diffindo

Severs or cuts an object.

Blasting curse

Reducto

Breaks apart or blasts through solid objects.

Specialist magic

Patronus charm

Expecto Patronum

Conjures a Patronus, a guardian powered by a happy memory, to defend against Dementors.

Memory charm

Obliviate

Alters or removes memories; misuse can cause serious harm.

Confundus charm

Confundo

Confuses a target and disrupts clear thinking.

Counter-spell

Finite

Ends or counters an active spell effect when applicable.

Water-making spell

Aguamenti

Conjures a stream of water.

Slow-fall charm

Arresto Momentum

Slows the movement of a falling person or object.

Control, concealment, and movement

Obstacle curse

Impedimenta

Slows or stops an approaching target, giving the caster time to escape or regroup.

Silencing charm

Silencio

Prevents a target from making sound.

Leg-locker curse

Locomotor Mortis

Locks a target’s legs together and restricts movement.

Incarcerous spell

Incarcerous

Conjures ropes that bind a target.

Revealing charm

Homenum Revelio

Detects the presence of people in an area.

Muffling charm

Muffliato

Fills nearby ears with a buzzing sound, making private conversations harder to overhear.

Mind magic and magical judgement

Mind-reading magic

Legilimens

Attempts to access thoughts, memories, or emotions in another mind.

Defence of the mind

Occlumency

Teaches the mind to resist Legilimency by controlling thoughts and emotions.

Boggart charm

Riddikulus

Turns a Boggart’s frightening form into something absurd, weakening it through laughter.

Growing charm

Engorgio

Enlarges an object or creature.

Shrinking charm

Reducio

Reduces the size of an object or creature.

Healing spell

Episkey

Mends certain minor injuries, such as a broken nose.

The Unforgivable Curses

These three curses are illegal because of the extreme harm they cause. Casting them is treated as a serious offence in the wizarding world, and their use reveals how magical skill without conscience becomes dangerous.

Control

Imperio

Attempts to control another person’s actions and choices.

Pain

Crucio

Inflicts extreme pain on the target.

Killing curse

Avada Kedavra

Causes instantaneous death and is one of the darkest spells in the wizarding world.

How spellcasting works

Words alone are not enough. The series shows that confidence, control, purpose, and emotional state can influence the outcome. A Patronus needs a powerful positive memory, while an Unforgivable Curse requires genuine intent to cause harm. This is why the same wand movement can have very different results in different hands.

The most memorable spells are not always the most powerful. Many define character: Harry’s Expelliarmus, Hermione’s preparation, Luna’s curiosity, and Neville’s courage all matter more than spectacle.
