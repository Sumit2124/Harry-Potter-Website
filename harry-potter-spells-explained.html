Spellbook guide

Harry Potter
spells explained

Spells reflect intention as much as technique. From defensive charms to everyday magic, each incantation tells part of the wizarding world’s story.

Archive note 01

Defensive magic

Expelliarmus disarms an opponent, Protego creates a shield, and Expecto Patronum summons a Patronus against Dementors.

Archive note 02

Useful everyday spells

Lumos creates light, Accio summons an object, Alohomora opens locks, and Reparo repairs broken objects.

Archive note 03

Unforgivable Curses

Imperio controls another person, Crucio inflicts pain, and Avada Kedavra kills. Their use carries severe magical and legal consequences.
