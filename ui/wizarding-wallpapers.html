Quick answers

Wallpaper questions,
answered.

Are these official Harry Potter images?

No. These are original AI-created atmospheric wallpapers made for this unofficial fan archive. They do not use book covers, actors, film stills, or official promotional artwork.

What format are the downloads?

Each download is a high-resolution PNG at 1672 × 941 pixels, suitable for desktop and tablet backgrounds. Mobile browsers may open the image first; use Save Image from the browser menu.

Can I share these wallpapers?

You may share the archive page with friends for personal fan use. Please keep the project context and creator attribution with the image, and do not sell or present the artwork as official material.
