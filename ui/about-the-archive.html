About the
Wizarding Archive

The Wizarding Archive is an unofficial fan-made project by Sumit Thapliyal. It is designed as a welcoming, searchable reference for people who want to revisit the Harry Potter books, films, characters, spells, creatures, locations, and themes.

Why this project exists

The archive combines structured reference pages with playful experiences: an interactive Hogwarts map, family tree, house and character activities, spell tools, Horcrux explanations, and a collection of original AI-created atmospheric wallpapers. The goal is to make the world easier to explore while keeping the interface calm, visual, and useful on mobile.

Each guide focuses on one question or topic, then links to the next natural part of the journey. A visitor can start with the movie order, follow a character into the story, investigate a spell, and continue into the place or event where it matters.

How the archive is created

Reference first

Focused explanations

Pages are written as original summaries and fan reference notes. They distinguish story facts from interpretation and avoid copying long passages from the books or films.

Built to explore

Connected experiences

Characters, books, Horcruxes, spells, creatures, and locations are connected through descriptive links so visitors can keep exploring rather than reach a dead end.

Original artwork

Wallpaper collection

The wallpaper gallery uses original AI-created atmospheric scenes made for this project. The images do not use book covers, actors, film stills, or official promotional artwork.
