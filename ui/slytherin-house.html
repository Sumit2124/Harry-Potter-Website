Hogwarts house guide

Slytherin House
explained

Slytherin is the Hogwarts house associated with ambition, resourcefulness, determination, and leadership. Founded by Salazar Slytherin, it is represented by a serpent and has a common room beneath the Black Lake.

What are Slytherin traits?

Slytherins are often strategic, self-directed, and willing to pursue a difficult goal. These traits are not automatically selfish or cruel: leadership, resilience, and ambition can be used to protect people or to control them.

The series explores this tension through characters such as Severus Snape, Draco Malfoy, Narcissa Malfoy, Horace Slughorn, and Voldemort. Their choices, rather than their house alone, determine what their ambition becomes.

Slytherin facts

Founder

Salazar Slytherin

Salazar valued ambition and magical heritage. His prejudice against Muggle-born students became part of Hogwarts' most dangerous legacy.

Symbol and colours

Serpent · green and silver

The serpent reflects Slytherin's association with Parseltongue, strategy, and survival. The house colours are green and silver.

Common room

Beneath the Black Lake

The Slytherin common room lies in the dungeons under the lake, giving it the atmospheric green light often associated with the house.

Head and ghost

Snape and the Bloody Baron

Severus Snape is head of house for much of Harry's time at Hogwarts. The Bloody Baron is the Slytherin ghost.

Famous Slytherin members

Severus Snape, Draco Malfoy, Narcissa Malfoy, Horace Slughorn, Merlin, and Tom Riddle are well-known Slytherins. The story's most important point is that Slytherin is not a moral verdict: Draco's hesitation, Narcissa's choice to protect her son, and Snape's long loyalty all complicate simple stereotypes.

Slytherin's values become dangerous only when ambition is separated from empathy. The house is a lens for choices, not a prediction of destiny.
