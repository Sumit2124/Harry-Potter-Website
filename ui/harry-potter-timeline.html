The Harry Potter
timeline explained

This guide follows the major turning points that shape the wizarding world: the school’s founding, Voldemort’s rise, Harry’s seven years at Hogwarts, and the final battle.

c. 10th century

Hogwarts is founded

Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw, and Salazar Slytherin establish Hogwarts to teach young witches and wizards safely. Their disagreement over who should be admitted eventually leads Slytherin to leave the school and create the Chamber of Secrets.

1926–1945

Tom Riddle’s school years

Tom Riddle grows up in a Muggle orphanage before arriving at Hogwarts. He charms teachers and students, discovers he is the Heir of Slytherin, opens the Chamber of Secrets, and begins experimenting with the magic that will create Horcruxes.

1970s

The first wizarding war

Voldemort gathers Death Eaters and spreads fear through the wizarding world. Dumbledore’s Order of the Phoenix resists him, while a prophecy suggests that a child born at the end of July may have the power to defeat him.

31 October 1981

The Boy Who Lived

Voldemort attacks the Potter family at Godric’s Hollow. Lily Potter’s sacrifice protects Harry, the Killing Curse rebounds, and Voldemort loses his body. Harry becomes famous before he is old enough to understand why.

1991–1992

First year: a hidden Stone

Harry learns he is a wizard, meets Ron and Hermione, and finds a home at Hogwarts. The trio prevents Voldemort from obtaining the Philosopher’s Stone, revealing that his enemy has been trying to return.

1992–1993

Second year: the Chamber reopens

Messages appear on the walls and students are attacked. Harry learns he can speak Parseltongue, saves Ginny Weasley from the Chamber, and destroys Tom Riddle’s diary—the first Horcrux to fall.

1993–1994

Third year: truth about the past

Sirius Black escapes Azkaban and appears to be hunting Harry. By the end of the year, Harry learns Sirius was betrayed by Peter Pettigrew and discovers a living connection to his parents’ history.

1994–1995

Fourth year: Voldemort returns

The Triwizard Tournament brings students from other schools to Hogwarts. A plot sends Harry to a graveyard, where Voldemort regains a body and openly returns to power.

1995–1996

Fifth year: resistance begins

The Ministry denies Voldemort’s return and appoints Dolores Umbridge at Hogwarts. Harry forms Dumbledore’s Army to teach defensive magic, while the struggle at the Ministry reveals the prophecy’s full importance.

1996–1997

Sixth year: the Horcrux mission

Dumbledore shows Harry memories of Voldemort’s past and explains the Horcruxes. The year ends with Dumbledore’s death, leaving Harry, Ron, and Hermione committed to finishing the hunt.

1997–1998

The hunt and the Battle of Hogwarts

The trio leaves school to destroy Horcruxes while Voldemort controls the Ministry. At Hogwarts, each remaining Horcrux is destroyed, Harry accepts that he must face Voldemort, and the final battle ends the war.

19 years later

The epilogue

The surviving characters send a new generation to Hogwarts. The journey returns to Platform Nine and Three-Quarters, showing a world rebuilt after conflict but still connected to its history.
