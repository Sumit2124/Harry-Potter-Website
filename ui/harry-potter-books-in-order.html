There are seven Harry Potter novels. Read them in publication order to follow Harry from his first Hogwarts letter through the Horcrux hunt and the Battle of Hogwarts.

Books and films are slightly different

The films follow the same overall order, but the novels provide more interior thought, history, and side plots. Read the books first if you want the fullest version of characters such as Ginny, Neville, Luna, and Dobby. Watch the films first if you prefer a visual introduction, then return to the books for the details the adaptations had to shorten.

Best first-time reading order: start with Philosopher’s Stone and read straight through to Deathly Hallows. The mysteries are designed to unfold in this sequence.
