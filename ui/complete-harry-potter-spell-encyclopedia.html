How to read this spell encyclopedia

Each entry gives the incantation, spell type, effect, and story meaning. Some spells are charms, some are curses, and some are broader magical disciplines. The labels help organize the archive, but the series often treats magic as a matter of focus, emotion, and moral choice.

Most searched spell questions

Signature spell

Why does Harry use Expelliarmus?

Because it matches his instinct to defend without killing. The spell becomes a moral marker, not just a combat habit.

Hardest charm

Why is Expecto Patronum difficult?

It requires a caster to hold a powerful happy memory while facing fear directly. Technique matters, but emotional focus matters more.

Dark magic

Why are three curses unforgivable?

They remove freedom, inflict torture, or take life. The law treats them as unforgivable because their usual purpose is deliberate harm.
