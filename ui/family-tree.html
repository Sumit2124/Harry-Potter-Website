02THE CONNECTIONS
01

Love is the oldest magic.

Lily Potter's sacrifice protects Harry before he can understand what family means. That protection becomes the first invisible thread running through the entire tree.

02

Chosen family is still family.

Ron, Hermione, Sirius, the Weasleys, and the wider Order give Harry a home that is built through loyalty, meals, arguments, and showing up when it matters.

03

Blood alone decides nothing.

The Black and Gaunt lines show how obsession with ancestry can become cruel. The story repeatedly asks what people do with their inheritance—not whether they deserve it.
