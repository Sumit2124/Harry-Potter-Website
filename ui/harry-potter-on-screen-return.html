Hogwarts returns
to the screen.

Harry Potter is returning in an all-new HBO Original television series based on the books. It is not a re-release of the original films; it is a new adaptation inviting another generation to discover the journey from the beginning.

The Wizarding Archive is celebrating the return by making it easy to revisit the characters, places, spells, and story threads that made Hogwarts feel like home.

What is returning—and what is new?

The original eight films remain a complete screen journey. The newly announced project is a separate television adaptation of the novels, with its own cast and creative approach. A long-form series has room to spend more time with school life, friendships, mysteries, classes, side characters, and the gradual changes that make each Hogwarts year different.

That is why the return is exciting for both longtime fans and newcomers: the main story can be experienced again while still leaving space for fresh interpretation.

Why this story still feels magical

Harry Potter works because its magic is built around recognisable feelings: finding friends, learning who you are, facing fear, making mistakes, and choosing what kind of person you want to become. Hogwarts is memorable not only for its moving staircases and spells, but because it gives its characters a place to belong.

The new adaptation creates another opportunity to revisit those ideas. The books contain a layered world of house rivalries, family history, magical creatures, hidden rooms, and moral choices that reward a return to the beginning.

The screen may change, but the question that made the story last remains the same: what do we choose when courage, friendship, and power pull in different directions?
