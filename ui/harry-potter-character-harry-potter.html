Character guide

Harry Potter
explained

Harry Potter is the central character of the series: an orphan who discovers he is a wizard, finds a chosen family at Hogwarts, and eventually chooses sacrifice over power to end Voldemort's war.

Harry Potter at a glance

Film actor

Daniel Radcliffe

Daniel Radcliffe portrays Harry across all eight Harry Potter films, following the character from his first year at Hogwarts to the final battle.

Hogwarts house

Gryffindor

Harry is sorted into Gryffindor after asking the Sorting Hat not to place him in Slytherin. His choices matter as much as his natural abilities.

Wand

Holly and phoenix feather

Harry's wand shares a phoenix-feather core connection with Voldemort's wand, making their confrontations unusually important.

Story role

The Boy Who Lived

Harry survives Voldemort's attack as a baby because of Lily Potter's sacrifice, a protection that shapes the entire story.

Harry's main story arc

Harry begins as a child who has been denied belonging and knowledge about his past. Hogwarts gives him friendship, mentors, and a place where he is seen. Each school year forces him to learn that bravery is not the same as acting alone.

By the final book, Harry understands that a fragment of Voldemort's soul lives inside him. He walks into the Forbidden Forest voluntarily, allowing Voldemort's curse to destroy that fragment. His decision is the opposite of Voldemort's fear of death.

Friends, family and rivals

Hermione Granger and Ron Weasley are Harry's closest friends and essential partners in every major mystery. Dumbledore guides him but also keeps difficult truths from him. Sirius, Lupin, Hagrid, the Weasleys, and Dobby give Harry versions of the family he lost.

Voldemort is Harry's enemy, but they are connected by prophecy, shared wand cores, and the accidental Horcrux. The series uses that connection to show that a person's choices—not a similar past or special ability—define who they become.

Harry's defining strength is not that he is the most powerful wizard. It is his ability to care about other people, accept help, and choose mercy when revenge would be easier.
