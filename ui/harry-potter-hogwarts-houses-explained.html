The Hogwarts handbook

Hogwarts Houses
explained

The four houses were created by the founders of Hogwarts to recognise different strengths. A house reflects the values a student chooses to develop—not a limit on who they can become.

01 · Godric Gryffindor

Gryffindor

Values: courage, nerve, determination, and chivalry.
Symbol and colours: lion; scarlet and gold.
Common room: Gryffindor Tower.
Known members: Harry Potter, Hermione Granger, Ron Weasley, Neville Longbottom, and Albus Dumbledore.

02 · Helga Hufflepuff

Hufflepuff

Values: loyalty, fairness, patience, and hard work.
Symbol and colours: badger; yellow and black.
Common room: near the Hogwarts kitchens.
Known members: Cedric Diggory, Nymphadora Tonks, Pomona Sprout, and Newt Scamander.

03 · Rowena Ravenclaw

Ravenclaw

Values: wisdom, wit, creativity, and learning.
Symbol and colours: eagle; blue and bronze.
Common room: Ravenclaw Tower, entered through a riddle.
Known members: Luna Lovegood, Cho Chang, Filius Flitwick, and Garrick Ollivander.

04 · Salazar Slytherin

Slytherin

Values: ambition, resourcefulness, leadership, and determination.
Symbol and colours: serpent; green and silver.
Common room: the dungeons beneath the lake.
Known members: Severus Snape, Draco Malfoy, Horace Slughorn, and Merlin.

How the Sorting works

The Sorting Hat considers a student’s abilities, values, choices, and sometimes their own preference. The founders left their wisdom in the Hat so it could continue sorting students long after they were gone.

A house is a starting point for identity, not a prediction of a person’s future. Characters throughout the story show courage, kindness, curiosity, and ambition across every house.

House life at Hogwarts

Each house has its own common room, head of house, ghost, and traditions. Students earn or lose house points through their actions, and the four houses compete for the House Cup at the end of the school year. House identity creates community, but friendship and rivalry regularly cross house boundaries.

House guides

Heads of house

During Harry’s years at Hogwarts, Minerva McGonagall leads Gryffindor, Pomona Sprout leads Hufflepuff, Filius Flitwick leads Ravenclaw, and Severus Snape leads Slytherin. They are teachers, disciplinarians, and advocates for their students.

House history

House ghosts

Gryffindor has Nearly Headless Nick; Hufflepuff has the Fat Friar; Ravenclaw has the Grey Lady, Helena Ravenclaw; and Slytherin has the Bloody Baron. Their stories preserve pieces of Hogwarts history.

Common rooms and entrances

Gryffindors enter through the portrait of the Fat Lady. Hufflepuffs use a hidden route near the kitchens. Ravenclaws answer a riddle at an enchanted knocker, while Slytherins enter their dungeon common room beneath the lake. These entrances reflect the personality each founder wanted their house to nurture.

The story repeatedly challenges house stereotypes: a brave Slytherin, a clever Hufflepuff, or a compassionate Gryffindor is never a contradiction. The strongest characters choose their values every day.
