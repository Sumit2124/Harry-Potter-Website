Hogwarts house guide

Gryffindor House
explained

Gryffindor is the Hogwarts house associated with courage, nerve, determination, and the willingness to act when something matters. It was founded by Godric Gryffindor and is represented by a lion.

What are Gryffindor traits?

Gryffindors are often described as brave, bold, protective, and quick to stand up for others. In the story, bravery is not simply fearlessness. Harry, Neville, Hermione, and Ron are afraid at important moments, but they choose to act despite that fear.

Those strengths can also become recklessness. The best Gryffindor characters learn that courage works best when it is combined with loyalty, preparation, and care for other people.

Gryffindor facts

Founder

Godric Gryffindor

One of Hogwarts' four founders, Godric valued courage and admitted students based on character rather than bloodline.

Symbol and colours

Lion · scarlet and gold

The lion represents boldness and leadership. Scarlet and gold make the house easy to recognise at Quidditch matches and the House Cup ceremony.

Common room

Gryffindor Tower

Students enter through the portrait of the Fat Lady. The warm tower room becomes Harry's first lasting sense of home at Hogwarts.

Head and ghost

McGonagall and Nick

Minerva McGonagall is head of house during Harry's time at school. Nearly Headless Nick is the resident Gryffindor ghost.

Famous Gryffindor members

Harry Potter, Hermione Granger, Ron Weasley, Neville Longbottom, Ginny Weasley, Albus Dumbledore, Minerva McGonagall, and Sirius Black are among the best-known Gryffindors. Their stories show different forms of bravery: study, sacrifice, leadership, resistance, and choosing compassion over revenge.

Being sorted into Gryffindor does not make a person heroic automatically. The house matters because its members repeatedly choose what they value when the pressure is highest.
