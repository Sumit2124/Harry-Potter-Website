A story about
the light within us.

Harry Potter is more than a story about a boy who lived. It is a journey through wonder and loss, a reminder that our choices define us far more than our origins ever could.

This little corner of the internet is made for revisiting the moments, characters, and ideas that still feel like home.

01THE PLOT
“

It is our choices, Harry, that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities.

— Albus Dumbledore

On his eleventh birthday, Harry learns he is a wizard and enters a world hidden in plain sight. At Hogwarts, he finds friendship in Ron and Hermione, discovers a talent for magic, and learns the truth about the dark wizard who tried to destroy him as a baby.

Across seven years, the trio grows from curious children into courageous young wizards. Together they face secrets, prophecies, sacrifice, and a war that asks every person to choose what they stand for.
