Character guide

Harry Potter
character guide

The wizarding world is shaped by choices: friendship, loyalty, courage, ambition, and the decision to resist fear.

Archive note 01

The golden trio

Harry, Hermione, and Ron bring different strengths to the fight: courage, knowledge, and loyalty. Their friendship is the emotional centre of the series.

Archive note 02

Teachers and mentors

Dumbledore, McGonagall, Lupin, Hagrid, and Snape each guide Harry in different ways, while carrying their own complicated histories.

Archive note 03

Allies and adversaries

The Weasleys, D.A., Order of the Phoenix, Malfoy family, and Death Eaters show how the war touches every part of wizarding society.
