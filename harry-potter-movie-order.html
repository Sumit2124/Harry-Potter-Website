Harry Potter
movie order

Watch the eight Harry Potter films in release order to follow Harry’s journey from his first Hogwarts letter to the Battle of Hogwarts.

Archive note 01

Films 1–3

Philosopher’s Stone (2001), Chamber of Secrets (2002), and Prisoner of Azkaban (2004) introduce Hogwarts, friendship, and Harry’s family history.

Archive note 02

Films 4–6

Goblet of Fire (2005), Order of the Phoenix (2007), and Half-Blood Prince (2009) show Voldemort’s return and reveal the Horcrux mission.

Archive note 03

Films 7–8

Deathly Hallows — Part 1 (2010) and Part 2 (2011) complete the Horcrux hunt and the final battle against Voldemort.
