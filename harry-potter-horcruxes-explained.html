Harry Potter
Horcruxes explained

A Horcrux is an object—or in Harry’s case, a person—used to contain a fragment of Voldemort’s soul. Each destruction made him more vulnerable.

Archive note 01

What is a Horcrux?

It is a magical vessel created through murder and a forbidden ritual. Splitting the soul lets a wizard survive bodily death, but severely damages their humanity.

Archive note 02

Why Voldemort made seven

Tom Riddle wanted a seven-part soul because he believed seven was the most powerful magical number. His plan created six deliberate Horcruxes and one accidental fragment in Harry.

Archive note 03

How they were destroyed

Basilisk venom, Fiendfyre, and the Sword of Gryffindor can destroy a Horcrux because they damage the vessel beyond magical repair.
